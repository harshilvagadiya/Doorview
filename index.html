About Us

Better Quality · Better Price · Better Service

ClearVue Door is an American manufacturer of glass and aluminum garage doors. We hand build each one to order, right here in Nashville, Tennessee. We believe that a garage door should be more than just a retractable front door. It should make your home more functional, feel more comfortable, and look more appealing from the street.
We started our company based on three non-negotiable concepts:

Better Quality

Quality

For years we have taken our competitors’ doors apart, to find out what they were doing right. After a few design changes and adding our own glass and hardware - we surpassed their quality.

Better Price

Price

We’ve spent a lot of time and money getting to this point. We are currently the only manufacturer in the garage door industry that makes their own panels, hardware, springs, and glass. Those extra efforts make our products much better and less costly, so that we can pass on the savings to our customers.

Better Service

Service

What used to be called customer service, is more like customer neglect. With endless telephone prompts and chatbots, most companies do everything they can to avoid talking to a customer. We’re from the south, and we have real live Tennesseans who are anxious to take your calls and answer your questions and emails.

From a single custom door to a full dealer order, every project gets the same care in fit, finish and quality control. Not sure which model or glass suits your climate and opening. Tell us about your space and we will point you to the right build.
